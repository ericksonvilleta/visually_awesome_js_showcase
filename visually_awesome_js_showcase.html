About Nexus Visuals

Welcome to your interactive JavaScript creative suite! This application showcases three distinct visual engines built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas, and modern CSS 3D transforms:

  • Quantum Constellation: Physics-based particle simulation with mouse attraction and dynamic distance triangulation.
  • Harmonic Kaleidoscope: Procedural mathematical art generator utilizing radial symmetry matrices.
  • Cybernetic Card: Responsive 3D tilt card with dynamic lighting, glowing specular highlights, and perspective depth.

Use the control panel sliders on the right to tweak live parameters and switch between modules seamlessly.
